# Marketplace Seller Standards

Sellers require account approval, contact/store identity, payout readiness, and acceptance of current seller, fee, returns, prohibited-items, and payout terms before first publication or sale. Listings must truthfully describe category, condition, defects, price, currency, inventory, media, fulfillment, location eligibility, and returns.

Off-platform payment circumvention, counterfeit goods, deceptive authenticity claims, spam/duplicate listings, harassment, manipulated reviews, and fulfillment misrepresentation are prohibited. Enforcement is explainable, audited, and appealable where policy permits.
