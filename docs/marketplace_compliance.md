# Marketplace Compliance

High-volume third-party seller thresholds and required disclosures must be implemented as versioned jurisdictional policy after legal approval. The workflow must collect and verify required identity, contact, bank, business, and tax information, maintain recertification, display required buyer disclosures, and suspend qualifying non-compliant sellers where required.

Sensitive identity, tax, and bank data is private, access-controlled, protected, and audited. Stripe or another payment settlement entity may own some tax-reporting obligations; engineering must store reporting responsibility and provider state without assuming PulseSoc is the filer. Legal review is required for tax, marketplace-facilitator, INFORM Act, consumer-protection, restricted-goods, and tax-reporting determinations.
