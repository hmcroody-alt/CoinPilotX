# PulseSoc Marketplace Constitution

The server is authoritative for seller/listing eligibility, inventory, price, delivery, tax, totals, orders, refunds, disputes, and payouts. Buying, standard selling, stores, and listings require no general Premium subscription or PulseSoc access fee. Every financial effect uses integer minor units, an immutable order snapshot, idempotency, reason codes, and audit history.

Orders may not enter payment until seller and listing approval, current price, currency, inventory reservation, delivery method, shipping, tax, and final total have been validated. Sponsored products remain policy-eligible and visibly labeled; organic relevance cannot be silently replaced by spend.

Policy families are versioned in `services/business_os/marketplace/policy.py`. Country/state tax, consumer-protection, marketplace-facilitator, high-volume-seller, restricted-goods, and tax-reporting determinations require legal/compliance review.
