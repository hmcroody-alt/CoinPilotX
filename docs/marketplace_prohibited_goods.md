# Marketplace Goods Policy

`MARKETPLACE_GOODS_V1` classifies listings as `ALLOWED`, `MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `RESTRICTED`, or `PROHIBITED`. Prohibited goods include illegal, stolen, counterfeit, recalled, exploitative, fraudulent, dangerous, controlled, credential/data, malware, trafficking, and unimplemented age-restricted goods.

Restricted/high-risk categories require explicit compliance and may require authenticity evidence or manual review. Sellers and rights holders may report listings; takedowns, seller actions, reasons, evidence references, actors, timestamps, and appeals are audited.
