# Marketplace Buyer Protection

Protection includes approved listings, secure server-authoritative checkout, price/inventory validation, disclosed shipping and tax, order tracking, eligible cancellation/return/refund paths, disputes, and seller accountability. It is not an absolute guarantee.

Checkout discloses seller/store, items, quantities, discounts, delivery method, shipping, tax, total, and payment method before authorization. Private pickup details are revealed only at the appropriate paid-order stage.
