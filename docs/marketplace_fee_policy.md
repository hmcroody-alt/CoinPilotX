# Marketplace Fee Policy

`MARKETPLACE_STANDARD_V1` proposes a 5% seller platform fee on merchandise net after seller-funded discounts. Tax, pass-through shipping, and buyer service fees are excluded. Buyer service, listing, standard monthly seller, and PulseSoc withdrawal fees are zero.

The policy is inactive unless all three production gates are satisfied: owner approval, seller-disclosure readiness, and an effective timestamp. Existing paid orders retain their snapshotted terms. PulseSoc absorbs ordinary Stripe processing cost under this launch proposal; gross platform fees must not be represented as net revenue before processor, refund, and dispute costs.

Partial merchandise refunds reverse the snapshotted fee proportionally; full merchandise refunds reverse it fully. No floating-point money is permitted.
