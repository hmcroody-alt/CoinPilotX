PulseSoc is a social platform with a marketplace built into it. You can follow people, post, comment, share reels and statuses, message and call other members, broadcast live, take part in groups, spaces and events, open a store, list products, and buy from other members.

PulseSoc is a social and commerce service. It is not a brokerage, exchange, bank, payment institution, escrow service, wallet provider, or investment adviser.

6. Messages, calls and live video

Direct messages and group chats on PulseSoc are not end-to-end encrypted. Connections are encrypted in transit, but message content is stored on our servers in a form our systems can read, which is what allows features such as search, moderation, abuse investigation, and the translation described below to work at all. Do not use PulseSoc messaging for information that requires end-to-end encryption.

Voice and video calls run through our realtime provider. Live broadcasts may be recorded and stored so they can be replayed later; if you broadcast, assume a recording exists.

7. Marketplace: who you are buying from

When you buy on PulseSoc you are buying from the seller named on the listing. The seller is responsible for the item, its description, its condition, its legality, and for getting it to you.

PulseSoc operates the platform and the checkout. For card orders, CoinPlotXAI Inc. is the merchant of record for the initial charge and pays the seller afterwards. Being merchant of record for the charge does not make PulseSoc the owner, manufacturer, importer, or inspector of the goods.

PulseDrop curates and publishes listings into PulseSoc surfaces. PulseDrop is not the seller, does not own the inventory, and is not the merchant of record for any order.

8. Prices and fees

The price you pay is shown in the order summary before you confirm. When you check out, PulseSoc records a priced quote for the order, including the commission rate in effect at that moment, and that quote is what the order is settled against.

  • Seller commission. PulseSoc currently charges sellers no commission — the rate in force today is 0%. A future rate takes effect only once it is approved and disclosed, and only for orders quoted after that point.
  • Buyer service fee. There is currently no buyer service fee.
  • Listing and subscription fees for sellers. There is currently no listing fee and no monthly seller fee.
  • Shipping. Buyers are not currently charged a separate shipping amount at checkout. If that changes, the amount will appear in the order summary before you pay.
  • Tax. PulseSoc does not currently calculate or collect sales tax at checkout, and your order record states that tax was not calculated. Do not treat a PulseSoc order summary as a tax determination.

9. Payment methods

Card payments are processed by Stripe. PulseSoc does not receive or store your full card number.

Paying by card requires two separate things to be true: card payments must be enabled on the platform, and the individual seller must have completed the onboarding our payment processor requires for their store. When either is not true, card payment is unavailable for that listing — so card availability is conditional, and neither "cards are unavailable" nor "cards always work" describes PulseSoc.

Some sellers offer cash on pickup, local pickup, or payment in person. Those payments happen directly between you and the seller. PulseSoc does not process, hold, escrow, or guarantee them, and PulseSoc charges no commission on them.

You can add items to a cart and complete a purchase as a guest, without creating an account. If you check out as a guest we still process the order details needed to fulfil the purchase.

10. Delivery

Delivery is arranged by the seller. Shipping destinations on PulseSoc are currently limited to the United States. PulseSoc does not currently offer worldwide shipping, and no PulseSoc order is currently fulfilled by a third-party supplier network.

Where an order ships, our payment processor collects the delivery address needed to fulfil it.
