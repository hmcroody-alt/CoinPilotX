Free to download. Requires iOS 15.1 or later. PulseSoc Premium is optional.

What you can do

Post and follow

Share text, photos and video, follow the people you care about, and reply in the open.

Reels

Short vertical video with sound, in a full-screen feed.

Live video

Go live from your phone, or watch and comment in someone else's stream.

Direct messages

One-to-one and group conversations, with photos, voice notes and files.

Voice and video calls

Call anyone you can message, with incoming calls that ring like a phone call.

Groups and rooms

Topic groups, plus live audio rooms for conversations that are not written down.

Creator profiles

A profile page that collects your posts, reels and links in one place.

Marketplace

List something for sale, or buy from a seller, with payment handled by Stripe.

Safety and control

Reporting, blocking and moderation are part of the product rather than a setting you have to find.

Report anything

Posts, profiles, messages and live streams all have a report control. Reports reach human review.

Block and mute

Block an account from its profile. Blocked accounts cannot message you or see your posts.

Choose who sees you

Posts can be public or limited, and you can keep your profile out of search results.

Moderation review

Content flagged by a person or by automated review is held pending a moderation decision.

Requirements and pricing

Price
The app is free to download and use.
PulseSoc Premium
An optional auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple ID. It renews until you cancel it in your Apple account settings.
Ad credit
An optional one-time purchase for people running promotions. Nothing else in the app requires a payment.
Device
iPhone running iOS 15.1 or later. There is no Android app today.
