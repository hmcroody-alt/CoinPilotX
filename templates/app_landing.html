Safety and control

Reporting, blocking and moderation are part of the product rather than a setting you have to find.

Report anything

Posts, profiles, messages and live streams all have a report control. Reports reach human review.

Block and mute

Block an account from its profile. Blocked accounts cannot message you or see your posts.

Choose who sees you

Posts can be public or limited, and you can keep your profile out of search results.

Moderation review

Content flagged by a person or by automated review is held pending a moderation decision.

Requirements and pricing

Price
The app is free to download and use.
PulseSoc Premium
An optional auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple ID. It renews until you cancel it in your Apple account settings.
Ad credit
