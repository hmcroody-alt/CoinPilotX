Why these pages say what is missing

Each page above describes what that part of PulseSoc does, and several of them spend a section on what it does not. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted; there are no hashtags; calls are one-to-one; Marketplace card payments are paused.

Those are the things people find out after installing, and finding them out then is worse for everyone than reading them here. A feature page that only lists capabilities is a page that has decided the reader is a target rather than a user.

The crypto side

PulseSoc grew out of a crypto intelligence product, and that part still exists: market analysis, scam and safety tooling, and portfolio tracking. The app carries a markets screen for the same reason.
