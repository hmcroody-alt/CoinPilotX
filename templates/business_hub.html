{# The web Business hub -- the browser counterpart of the Business tile on the mobile Profile grid. Every card's state comes from `services/business_web_sections.resolve()`, which derives it from `services/app_links.py` and the live Flask url_map. This template therefore contains no product copy about what works: the labels and blurbs are mirrored from `mobile-native/src/api/businessOs.ts`, and the four state sentences live in `business_web_sections.STATE_NOTES`. If a card is wrong, the registry is wrong, and there is one place to fix it. Palette is black / white / green, per the standing rule for business surfaces. Green marks only the cards that genuinely work in a browser, which is the one thing a member is here to find out; everything else is greyscale. Using colour to mean "available" rather than as decoration is why the summary line at the top can be read at a glance. Deliberately NOT a grid of fourteen links. Three of these sections have a Flask route that answers 200 and is withheld on purpose, two exist only inside the app, and two are not built at all -- so a uniform link grid would be a page that lies about eight of its fourteen cards. The card states are the content. A FRAGMENT, not a document. `pulse_social_shell()` in bot.py builds the surrounding page -- navigation, the signed-in header, the app CTA -- and already redirects an anonymous visitor to the login page, so this file renders only the hub's own markup and inherits the auth behaviour of every sibling /pulse page rather than restating it. #}
