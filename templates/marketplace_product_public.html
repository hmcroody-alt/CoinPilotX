WHY THIS TEMPLATE EXISTS AT ALL ------------------------------- `/pulse/marketplace/` already rendered a complete product page, through `pulse_social_shell`. That shell calls `require_account()` itself and 302s, so it can never serve a logged-out reader no matter what the route does: the redirect was in two places, not one. It is also the signed-in app chrome -- a left nav with Messenger, Settings and Log Out -- which is the right frame for a member and the wrong frame for someone arriving from a search result who has no account yet. So a signed-in member keeps the shell they had, and this template serves everyone else. One URL, one canonical, two frames. The alternative -- a second product path for the public version -- would split one product's ranking signal across two URLs and make the canonical tag a coin toss.
