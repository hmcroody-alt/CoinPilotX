# CJ API requirements

Current official documentation checked 2026-09-07. Documentation inventory only; no authenticated API behavior is claimed.

## Counting and classification

**126 distinct method+URL operations**: 25 MVP, 23 production-hardening, 74 optional, 4 not needed for the selected flow. One shared payBalanceV2 endpoint is counted once even though documented twice. Different methods on product/conn/connection count separately. A local webhook receiver and composite normalized methods are not additional CJ endpoints. These are implementation-footprint counts, not calls per order.

T1 includes token refresh and all four webhook-management endpoints: neither lifecycle nor reliable updates may be deferred merely to label an unsafe demo “MVP.” T2 includes recovery/sandbox/dispute and optional-in-use sourcing/advanced quote support; implement applicable recovery before production. T3 is intentionally outside the ordinary CJ-carrier dropshipping MVP. T4 contains older/alternative operations replaced in this design, not a claim that CJ has removed all of them.

**Selected MVP**: merchant-owned account, raw CJ product flow1, V2 create-only, staged cart/parent preparation, explicit merchant balance funding, CJ carrier fulfillment (shopLogisticsType2), detailed freight quotation, subscribed product updates and owned shop validation. No print-on-demand/private stock/platform-waybill workflow is implied.

## Authentication, rates and response rules

- CJ = `CJ-Access-Token` header. CJ+JSON also requires `Content-Type: application/json`. Token exchange/refresh use the JSON secret specified, not an invented bearer/OAuth header. All primary URLs below are HTTPS and case-sensitive.
- **R** means intersect applicable limits: 10 requests/s/IP; no more than 3 users/IP; documented non-login ceiling 30/s (interaction unspecified); account Free/level0–1=1/s, Plus/2=2/s, Prime/3=4/s, Advanced/4–5=6/s; actual settings may impose further URL quotas. Do not treat sample qpsLimit100 as a grant. Ticket family 1/s and batch order detail/proof of delivery 2/account/s also apply; stricter effective limits win pending CJ clarification.
- Points: base50,000/day plus conversion, minute replenishment, UTC reset; unlisted endpoints cost0 under the general policy but still consume rate capacity. Unavailable Shipping Methods says10 points in its endpoint documentation while absent from the cost table: conservatively budget10 and ask CJ. Old List's1000-calls/day prose conflicts with the new points policy.
- No unspecified batch/page maximum below is unlimited. Keep bounded internal defaults and confirm a contract before expanding. “Length” fields on arrays are not automatically proven item-count limits.
- Retry read-only queries with bounded jitter after transient failure, respecting points/rate windows. Reconcile every ambiguous mutation before retry. No general Retry-After guarantee, arbitrary burst allowance, or exactly-once payment/order guarantee is documented.
- Common envelope: HTTP/code plus result/success and operation data, requestId for diagnostics. Review/video examples instead show code0; use endpoint-specific adapters after contract validation, not a global code0 success rule. Some fields/examples conflict; [evidence notes](catalog_evidence.md) and [order evidence](orders_evidence.md) record these gaps.

## Deliberately absent capabilities

No dedicated API is invented for local product import, customer checkout/payment/refund, atomic catalog-stock reservation, guaranteed landed-price locking, general paid-order cancellation, hosted SaaS OAuth consent, automatic CJ-wallet top-up, webhook replay history or account/submerchant creation. Composite mappings are in [supplier contract](PULSESOC_SUPPLIER_CONTRACT.md). Shop store-product connections are optional for raw CJ flow1; My Product membership is not proven mandatory. Generic interface-definition examples such as setting/account/set are not treated as a current supported Settings capability without an actual endpoint contract.

This table inventories optional operations sufficiently to classify and route further work; it does not make their undocumented fields, side effects, privileges or units implementation-ready. Full forensic findings and open questions are in [report](CJ_DROPSHIPPING_FORENSIC_REPORT.md).
